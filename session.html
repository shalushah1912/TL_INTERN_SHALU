Date26 May 2026
VenueTinkerers' Lab, Sanjivani University
ParticipantsSummer Internship Program 2026 Participants

Topics Covered

  • 1
    Understanding Design ThinkingIntroduction to principles, importance of innovation, and human-centered approach to problem-solving.
  • 2
    Empathy & User UnderstandingUnderstanding user needs, conducting observations and interviews, building empathy for effective solutions.
  • 3
    Problem IdentificationDefining real-world problems, analyzing root causes, framing problem statements effectively.
  • 4
    Idea GenerationBrainstorming techniques, encouraging creativity, generating multiple solution approaches.
  • 5
    Prototyping & TestingDeveloping simple prototypes, gathering user feedback, improving solutions through iteration.
  • 6
    Innovation & EntrepreneurshipTransforming ideas into practical solutions, developing an innovation mindset for startups and businesses.

  • Innovate — Generate meaningful, impactful ideas and encourage experimentation.
  • Empathize — Understand people and solve real-world problems through user-centered design.
  • Implement — Convert ideas into actionable solutions that create impact.

Key Takeaways

  • Innovation begins with understanding people.
  • Creativity can be developed through structured thinking.
  • Design Thinking encourages collaboration and experimentation.
  • Failure is an opportunity for learning and improvement.
  • Applicable in academics, business, engineering, and entrepreneurship.




Learning Outcomes

Understand the Design Thinking process Apply empathy-based problem solving Develop innovative solutions Enhance teamwork & collaboration Improve critical & analytical thinking Build confidence for real-world challenges

"Innovation begins when we understand people, think creatively, and act courageously."

3 Session Three

Intellectual Property Rights

Protect Your Innovations. Own Your Ideas. Grow with Them.

MS

Dr. M. Sujith

Innovation Leader & IPR Expert

Intellectual Property Rights Innovation Management Patent Filing Technology Transfer Entrepreneurship Development

Session Details

Date10 June 2026
Time12:30 PM Onwards
VenueTinkerers' Lab, Sanjivani University
ParticipantsStudents, Faculty Members, Innovators

Types of Intellectual Property Rights

Patents

Protect new inventions and technological innovations. Provide exclusive rights to inventors for a specific period.

Copyrights

Protect literary, artistic, musical, and software creations. Prevent unauthorized copying and distribution.

Trademarks

Protect brand names, logos, symbols, and slogans. Help distinguish products and services in the market.

Industrial Designs

Protect the visual appearance and aesthetic design of products.

Trade Secrets

Protect confidential business information and proprietary processes.

Topics Covered

  • 1
    IPR FundamentalsMeaning, significance, and the need for protecting innovative ideas.
  • 2
    Patent Filing ProcessSteps involved in patent registration, documentation, and eligibility requirements.
  • 3
    Copyright ProtectionRights of creators and authors; protection of digital and creative content.
  • 4
    Trademark RegistrationImportance of branding and the trademark application process.
  • 5
    Innovation & EntrepreneurshipCommercialization of inventions and the role of IPR in startups.
  • 6
    Legal Protection & CompliancePreventing infringement and understanding intellectual property laws.

Key Takeaways

  • Ideas and innovations are valuable assets.
  • IPR protects creators and innovators.
  • Patents help secure technological inventions.
  • Trademarks strengthen brand identity.
  • IPR management can generate business opportunities.

Q&A Highlights

  • How to apply for a patent.
  • Patentability criteria for student projects.
  • Copyright for software and digital content.
  • Trademark registration procedures.
  • IP ownership in collaborative projects.
  • Costs and timelines of patent filing.


"Ideas are powerful. Protect them. Own them. Grow with them."

Theme: Be an Innovator. Be IP Smart.
