☀️ Light Sensor (LDR / Photodiode)

Photoelectric effect — converting light into electrical signal

①
Light hits sensor
Photons strike the semiconductor surface
②
Electron excitation
Photons knock electrons free (photoelectric effect)
③
Current flows
Free electrons create measurable current / resistance change
④
Voltage output
ADC converts to lux reading via voltage divider
LDR: Resistance ↓ as Light ↑  |  Photodiode: Current ↑ as Light ↑  |  Lux = f(Voltage)
