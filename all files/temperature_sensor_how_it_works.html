🌡️ Temperature Sensor (NTC Thermistor / RTD)

Resistance changes with temperature — converting heat into electrical signal

①
Heat excites atoms
Thermal energy causes lattice vibrations in the material
②
Resistance changes
More heat = more collisions = resistance rises (PTC) or falls (NTC)
③
Voltage divider
Changing resistance shifts output voltage proportionally
④
ADC reads °C
Microcontroller converts voltage to temperature via Steinhart equation
NTC Thermistor: Resistance ↓ as Temp ↑  |  RTD (PT100): Resistance ↑ as Temp ↑  |  T = f(R)
