💧 Humidity Sensor (DHT11)

Capacitive sensing — measuring moisture in air

①
Air intake
Humid air enters through sensor mesh grill
②
Absorption
Polymer film absorbs water molecules from air
③
Capacitance change
More moisture → higher dielectric → more capacitance
④
Digital output
IC converts reading to digital signal on DATA pin
Relative Humidity (%)  =  (Capacitance measured)  →  mapped 0–100% RH
