Here is a simplified summary of how a Cricut machine works:

What it is

A smart craft cutter** that acts like a printer, but uses a tiny blade to cut out shapes and designs instead of printing ink.

What you need

The Machine: The device that does the cutting.

The App: Software on your phone or computer where you pick or draw your design.

The Mat: A sticky plastic mat that holds your material flat and in place.

The Material: What you are cutting (like vinyl stickers, iron-on patches, or cardstock paper).

How to use it

1. Design: Choose or create a design in the app.

2. Prep:Stick your material flat onto the sticky mat.

3. Cut: Load the mat into the machine, choose your material in the app, and press **Start**.

4. Finish: Peel away the extra material around your cut design and apply it to your project.





Core Components Needed to Work To operate the machine, you typically use a combination of three components: The Machine: Connects to your device wirelessly via Bluetooth or via a USB cable. Design Space Software: The official software (free to download on iOS, Android, Windows, and Mac) where you layout, type, or import the images you want to cut. The Cutting Mat: A flexible plastic mat with a sticky surface that holds your raw material completely flat and securely in place while the machine moves and cuts it.

the two trees TTC-450 is a high-performance 3D printer designed for professiionals, makers, and advanted hobbyists. it offers a large build volume, high precision, and a range of features that make it ideal for prototyping, manufacturing, and creative projects.


Cutting Tool / End Mill Information for TwoTrees TTC450 CNC Router

The TwoTrees TTC450 uses an ER11 collet spindle, which can hold cutting tools (end mills) with diameters from 0.5 mm to 7 mm. The newer TTC450 Ultra comes with a 500 W spindle that can run between 8,000 and 30,000 RPM.

Standard Tools Included with TTC450 Many TTC450 kits include: ER11 collets (3.175 mm and 6 mm) 10 × V-shaped engraving cutters (30°)


The Two Trees TTC450 uses stepper motors to move the machine accurately along the X, Y, and Z axes. These motors convert electrical pulses into precise mechanical movement, allowing the CNC machine to engrave and cut accurately.

  • Stepper Motor Specifications

  • Motor Model: 17HS8401S

  • Motor Type: NEMA 23 Stepper Motor

  • Driver: A4988 Stepper Driver

  • Rated Current: 1.3 A

  • Torque: Approximately 20 mN·m (as listed by manufacturer specifications)


X-Axis Assembly in Two Trees TTC450 The X-axis assembly is the horizontal movement system of the TTC450 CNC machine. It carries the Z-axis and spindle assembly from left to right across the machine.
