Here is a simplified summary of how a Cricut machine works:

What it is

A smart craft cutter** that acts like a printer, but uses a tiny blade to cut out shapes and designs instead of printing ink.

What you need

The Machine: The device that does the cutting.

The App: Software on your phone or computer where you pick or draw your design.

The Mat: A sticky plastic mat that holds your material flat and in place.

The Material: What you are cutting (like vinyl stickers, iron-on patches, or cardstock paper).

How to use it

1. Design: Choose or create a design in the app.

2. Prep:Stick your material flat onto the sticky mat.

3. Cut: Load the mat into the machine, choose your material in the app, and press **Start**.

4. Finish: Peel away the extra material around your cut design and apply it to your project.





Core Components Needed to Work To operate the machine, you typically use a combination of three components: The Machine: Connects to your device wirelessly via Bluetooth or via a USB cable. Design Space Software: The official software (free to download on iOS, Android, Windows, and Mac) where you layout, type, or import the images you want to cut. The Cutting Mat: A flexible plastic mat with a sticky surface that holds your raw material completely flat and securely in place while the machine moves and cuts it.
